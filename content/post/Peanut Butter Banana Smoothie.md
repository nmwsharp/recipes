From [Budget Bytes](https://www.budgetbytes.com/peanut-butter-banana-smoothie/). Serves 1. Can easily double in most blenders.

- 1 frozen banana
- 2 Tbsp creamy peanut butter
- 1 tsp brown sugar
- 1/4 tsp vanilla extract
- 1 cup milk

Blend until smooth.
