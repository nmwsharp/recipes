Adapted from [NYT Cooking (Eric Kim)](https://cooking.nytimes.com/recipes/1026190-sausage-and-apple-cornbread-dressing). Serves ~8.

## Ingredients

- unsalted butter, for the dish
- 1 sweet-tart red apple (Pink Lady or Honeycrisp), sliced into 1/3-inch triangles
- 2 Tbsp apple cider vinegar
- 6 Tbsp unsalted butter, divided
- 1 large yellow onion, finely diced
- salt and pepper
- 1/4 cup finely chopped fresh sage
- 2 cups chicken broth, divided
- 12 oz sweet or spicy Italian sausage, casings removed
- 1 lb cornbread, crumbled (about 4 cups; or 1-2 boxes Jiffy Corn Muffin Mix, need eggs+milk, see note)
- 1 (4 oz) sleeve saltine crackers, crushed (about 2 cups)
- 1/2 cup dried cranberries

### Spices

- 1/2 tsp fennel seeds
- 1 tsp celery seed
- 1 tsp coriander seed
- 1 tsp dried thyme
- 1/2 tsp crushed red pepper

*If you have a mortar and pestle, grind the whole seeds together before adding.*

## Directions

Heat oven to 350°F. Generously butter a shallow 2.5-quart casserole dish.

Toss the sliced apple with the vinegar in a small bowl and set aside.

In a large skillet over medium heat, melt 5 Tbsp butter. Add the onion, season with salt and pepper, and cook, stirring occasionally, until translucent and just starting to brown, 8-10 minutes. Add the spices and cook, stirring constantly, until fragrant, about 1 minute. Turn off the heat and stir in the sage. Add 1 cup broth and scrape up any stuck-on bits. Transfer to a large bowl.

In the now-empty skillet over medium-high, cook the sausage, breaking it up, until no longer pink, about 5 minutes. Add 1/4 cup broth, bring to a simmer, then remove from heat. Add to the large bowl.

Drain the apples. Add half the apples, the dried cranberries, cornbread, and saltines to the bowl. Toss until everything is moistened, adding more broth as needed — you should be able to squeeze a fistful and have it mostly cohere, not dripping wet. Taste and adjust seasoning.

Spread the stuffing evenly in the buttered dish. Melt the remaining 1 Tbsp butter and toss with the remaining apples, then scatter on top. *(Can be covered with foil and refrigerated up to 3 days. Add 1/4 cup broth before baking if it looks dry.)*

Cover with foil and bake until steamed and fragrant, about 30 minutes. Remove foil and bake another 10-15 minutes until crisp on top and browned in spots. Serve warm.

## Notes

**Cornbread:** Jiffy Corn Muffin Mix works well, make according to package instructions. 1 box makes a smaller, more sausagey stuffing; 2 boxes gives more volume and a more balanced cornbread-to-sausage ratio. Avoid super-sweet cornbread.
